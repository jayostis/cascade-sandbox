# Notices for the demo hospitals' data

Every patient here is synthetic. `patients/pt-*.json` and `patients/s-*.json`
were written by hand; `patients/syn-*.json` were generated by Synthea and
post-processed as `../synthea/README.md` says.

## Synthea

The `syn-*` records are output of [Synthea](https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea)
v4.0.0, by The MITRE Corporation, released under the Apache License 2.0. Its
people are simulated; none is a real person. The real provider names,
addresses, phone numbers and identifiers that Synthea draws from public CMS
data were removed or replaced with the demo hospitals' own names.

## SNOMED CT (SNOMED International)

The records carry SNOMED CT concept identifiers and their terms: in the
generated records, exactly as Synthea's output writes them, under Synthea's
licence terms; in the hand-written ones, a few chosen by hand. What was
checked: they are concept identifiers and terms in codings only. There are no
relationships, hierarchies, refsets, maps or description sets here. Whether
each concept is in a particular subset, such as the Global Patient Set, was not
checked.
